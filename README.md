## Organisation overview
•	Name: Bra Zambi the Barber
•	Mission Statement: To provide high-quality, trendy grooming services that build self-confidence and foster genuine community connection in a modern, welcoming environment.
•	Vision Statement: To become the premier local grooming destination across Gauteng township communities by setting high standards of customer service and modern styling.

## Website goals and objectives
•	Specific Goals:
•	Establish a strong online brand presence.
•	Clear display of all service offerings alongside transparent pricing.
•	Enable direct online appointment bookings and inquiry submissions.
•	Showcase real client testimonials and high-resolution transformations.
•	Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
•	Achieve at least 500 unique web visits per month within the first quarter.
•	Convert 15% of total site visitors into online bookings or direct inquiries.
•	Reduce phone-in service inquiry volume by 30% through comprehensive online FAQs.
